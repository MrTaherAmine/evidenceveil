# Residual-Risk Methodology

Audit findings consider residual identifier/secret-like patterns and untransformed free text. Release statuses are `blocked`, `review-required`, or `eligible-for-controlled-review`. No status means legal anonymity or unconditional safe sharing. The CLI can record user-selected quasi-identifier field names for review, but v1.0 does not compute k-anonymity or equivalence classes for heterogeneous evidence sets.
