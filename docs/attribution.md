## Where attribution appears

Project identity is intentionally present in the CLI (`--help`, `version`, `about`, and `doctor`), package metadata, manifests, offline HTML reports, executive summaries, technical reports (through manifest metadata), restoration manifests, README, NOTICE, AUTHORS, and CITATION.cff.

Attribution is **not** inserted into the sanitized evidence payload under `sanitized/`. This preserves provenance and project credit without modifying the user’s transformed telemetry with unrelated author metadata.
